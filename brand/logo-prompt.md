# BLINDSPOT — logo prompts

Palette (all variants): paper `#F2F0EA` (background), ink `#121316` (main form), blind-spot amber `#FF7A00` (the warning lamp in a car side mirror, used as the only accent).
Constraints: one bold silhouette, readable at 32px, no text, no gradients beyond soft studio lighting, square 1:1, centered, generous margin.

---

## Variant A — Solid ink eye, amber slash

```
A 3D rendered app icon of a crossed-out eye: a bold, simple almond-shaped eye with a round iris, sculpted as a single solid object in deep ink black (#121316) with a soft satin finish, crossed by one thick diagonal bar from top-left to bottom-right in glowing blind-spot amber (#FF7A00), like the warning lamp in a car side mirror. The bar sits slightly in front of the eye and casts a soft shadow onto it. Soft studio lighting from the top left, gentle ambient occlusion, a faint contact shadow beneath. Plain warm paper background (#F2F0EA), no texture. Minimal, chunky geometry, rounded edges, thick strokes, no fine detail, readable at 32 pixels. Centered, square 1:1, large margin. No text, no extra elements.
```

```
A 3D rendered logo mark: a thick, bold eye shape in ink black (#121316) that has been physically cut in two by a diagonal gap. The two halves are offset slightly along the cut, and the open space between them glows blind-spot amber (#FF7A00), like light from a car mirror warning lamp. ---
```

Notes for picking: A is the safest favicon. B tells the "car mirror" story best but carries more detail, so check it at 32px. C is the most ownable shape. At 16–32px drop the glow and keep flat ink + amber.
